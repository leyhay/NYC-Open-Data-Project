Swimming pools can be disgusting, especially if shared with others. You may not know what they do inside. This data set helps you check which pools have been inspected, their dates, and the location.

- The data set I'm using in this webpage is the NYC Pool Inspections. Department of Health and Mental Hygiene (DOHMH) provided the information being used. The local government agency is responsible for public health, disease prevention, and mental health services in New York City. It is one of the oldest and largest public health agencies in the world.

- The data set is about the pool inspections and if they have any outstanding issues. This is important because summer is coming up and knowing if your pool is clean or not is important for the safety of everyone. A permit is required to install, operate or construct any indoor or outdoor bathing establishment with a pool in New York City. This dataset contains results of indoor and outdoor pool inspections. Without the proper certification and inspection, people can risk their health and this can sometimes be lofe threatening.
